# Tauheed Textile — Pakistan-First E-Commerce Website Master Plan

**Project type:** Premium Pakistani women’s clothing and textile e-commerce store  
**Primary audience:** Women shopping from Pakistan, plus overseas Pakistanis buying Pakistani formalwear  
**Working brand name:** Tauheed Textile  
**Primary outcome:** Turn collection browsing into confident purchases with a quick, mobile-first, trustworthy checkout.

---

## 1. Direction confirmed from the supplied material

The video and voice note show a collection of competitor/reference sites. They are **reference material only**—not designs to copy. The voice note asks the team to:

- review all shared examples and links;
- identify which options are useful and which are unnecessary;
- avoid duplicate/overlapping functionality;
- improve on the references where a better feature or experience is appropriate; and
- produce a plan for review before development begins.

The visible requirements are: category-led shopping, product grids with filter/sort, quick view, product zoom, a side cart, sale and “load more” sections, top reviews, login/register, product video links, Pakistani checkout/payment options, delivery and international-order information, order tracking, and WhatsApp assistance.

## 2. Brand position and customer promise

### Positioning

**Tauheed Textile brings refined Pakistani lawn, chiffon, pret, festive and bridal-ready pieces to customers who want clear product information, genuine quality and dependable delivery.**

The site should feel polished and premium, but not intimidating or overly ornate. It must feel equally natural on a phone in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar or Quetta.

### Brand pillars

1. **Pakistani craft, clearly presented** — fabric, embroidery and what is included are never vague.
2. **Confidence before checkout** — real photos, video, size guidance, review proof and visible delivery/return rules.
3. **Modern service** — fast mobile browsing, COD where available, local wallets/cards and prompt WhatsApp support.
4. **Quiet luxury** — elegant editorial visuals, restrained type and one consistent gold-and-black signature.

### Voice

Warm, direct and trustworthy. Use plain English with optional Urdu support; avoid aggressive “buy now” language.

- Primary line: **Pakistani elegance, thoughtfully made.**
- Supporting line: **From everyday lawn to statement occasion wear.**
- Trust line: **Clear details. Genuine quality. Delivery across Pakistan.**

## 3. Logo system

### Recommended logo direction

Use a **refined monogram + wordmark**, inspired by the supplied black-and-gold Tauheed Textile mark—not an imitation of a competitor logo.

- **Mark:** Interlock `T` and `T` inside a delicate circular or oval seal. Add a subtle thread/spool or paisley-inspired line only if it remains legible at 24 px.
- **Wordmark:** `TAUHEED` in an elegant high-contrast serif; `TEXTILE` underneath in spaced sans-serif capitals.
- **Avoid:** literal sewing-machine icons, generic crown icons, overly detailed calligraphy and thin gold strokes that disappear on mobile.

### Required logo files

- Full logo, black on transparent background.
- Full logo, ivory on black background.
- Gold foil version for premium packaging/social use only.
- Square monogram/favicon.
- Single-colour print version for invoices, labels and courier slips.

### Clear-space and use rules

- Keep logo clear space equal to the height of the `T` monogram.
- Use the black logo on ivory/white; use ivory logo on black/charcoal.
- Use the gold version only on dark hero imagery, luxury packaging or a dark footer—never on white body text.

## 4. Visual language and colour palette

### Core palette

| Role | Colour | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ink Black | Noir | `#12110F` | Header, footer, primary text, primary buttons |
| Warm Ivory | Cotton | `#F8F5EF` | Main page background, calm premium space |
| Soft Sand | Dastkaar | `#E8E0D4` | Cards, product detail surfaces, dividers |
| Antique Gold | Zari | `#B28A3E` | Logo accent, selected states, restrained highlights |
| Deep Olive | Mehndi | `#344238` | Secondary accent, seasonal editorial panels |
| Rose Clay | Gulab | `#B66D69` | Small sale/status accent—not a dominant UI colour |
| Success Green | In Stock | `#27764D` | Availability and confirmed order states |
| Alert Maroon | Limited | `#8B3C43` | Low stock and validation errors |

### Usage rule

Use **black + ivory as 85% of the interface**. Gold is a premium accent, not a background colour. Rose clay and olive appear only in editorial campaigns, tags and small highlights. This keeps the site elegant and avoids the inconsistent colours seen across many fashion stores.

### Type and layout

- **Headlines:** `Cormorant Garamond` or `Playfair Display`—high contrast but readable.
- **UI/body:** `Manrope`, `Inter` or `DM Sans`—clear at mobile sizes.
- **Urdu fallback:** `Noto Nastaliq Urdu` only for large editorial Urdu text; use `Noto Sans Arabic` for compact UI labels.
- **Desktop grid:** 12 columns, 1,280 px max content width.
- **Mobile grid:** 2 product columns, 16 px outer gutter, 12 px product gutter.
- **Photography:** Neutral daylight or soft studio light; clean backgrounds; no heavily filtered catalogue images.

## 6. Page-by-page experience

### Home page

1. **Announcement bar:** “Nationwide delivery • Secure payments • WhatsApp support.” Add a campaign message only when genuine.
2. **Hero:** One full-width campaign image/video, one clear collection title and one CTA: “Shop the Collection.” Never put a carousel with five competing messages above the fold.
3. **Shop by category:** Circular or softly rounded editorial cards for Lawn, Chiffon, Pret, Wedding and Unstitched.
4. **New arrivals:** Four products desktop / two columns mobile; “View all.”
5. **Watch & Buy:** Short vertical product videos with product names, prices and a direct add-to-cart route.
6. **Occasion edit:** “Everyday Elegance,” “Eid Edit,” “Wedding Guest” or current seasonal story.
7. **Best sellers / recently viewed:** Personalised only after enough data exists; otherwise use a curated best-seller row.
8. **Trust strip:** Genuine products, nationwide delivery, secure checkout, easy support.
9. **Customer stories:** 3–6 verified reviews with photos where consent exists.
10. **Newsletter:** “First access to new collections and private sale previews.”

### Collection / product-list page

- Large collection title, one-line description and count.
- Sticky filter/sort bar on mobile.
- Filters: category, ready-to-wear vs unstitched, size, colour family, price, availability, fabric, work/embellishment, occasion and delivery status.
- Sort: newest, price low-high, price high-low, best selling.
- Product card: primary image, optional hover/second image, wish-list, **Quick View eye icon**, label, product name, price, crossed-out original price when real, and stock status.
- Use **Load more** or numbered pagination—not infinite scroll—so users can reach the footer, compare items and return to their place.
- Quick View should show photos, price, sizes, what is included, stock and Add to Cart. It must not become a duplicate product page.

### Product detail page

- Breadcrumbs, clear product title, price in **PKR**, sale price/original price, availability.
- Responsive image gallery with full-screen pinch/zoom; desktop zoom-on-hover.
- Product video button: Instagram Reel, TikTok or YouTube embed/link only where a real product demo exists.
- Variant selectors: size, colour, stitched/unstitched configuration where applicable.
- Explicit package contents: e.g., “3-piece: embroidered lawn shirt, printed trouser, chiffon dupatta.”
- Fabric, work, measurements, model height/size, care instructions and SKU.
- Quantity, Add to Cart, Buy Now and Wishlist.
- **Delivery panel on every product page:** dispatch estimate, nationwide delivery policy, sale-item delivery charge rule, COD availability and international-order link.
- Accordions: description, size guide, shipping, returns/exchanges, care, reviews.
- Related products: 4–8 truly related items based on fabric/occasion/price; not a generic random slider.

### Cart and checkout

- **Cart drawer:** slides in from the side after Add to Cart. Shows line items, quantity, subtotal, free-shipping progress (if policy uses it), “Checkout” and “Continue shopping.”
- Cart page remains available for editable details, discount code and delivery estimate.
- Guest checkout first; account creation offered after purchase, never forced.
- Pakistan address: full name, mobile number, address, area, city, province, optional landmark. Validate Pakistani phone format without making a user retype it unnecessarily.
- Delivery method and clear ETA before payment.
- Payment choices: COD where eligible; card/wallet gateway; bank transfer with order reference and proof-upload route only if operationally supported. Do not show a payment method until it is live.
- Place Order button shows final PKR total and method. Confirmation includes order number, WhatsApp help, tracking link and delivery expectations.

### Account and tracking

- Passwordless mobile OTP or secure email/password registration.
- My Orders: timeline from order received → confirmed → packed → dispatched → delivered.
- Tracking page accepts order number + mobile/email; never expose order details through a guessable public URL.
- Wishlist, saved addresses, return/exchange request (when eligible) and review invitation after delivery.

## 7. Pakistan-specific operations

### Payment plan

- Make **Cash on Delivery** the default option where risk rules permit.
- Add a merchant-approved Pakistani gateway for cards and wallet payments. Easypaisa and JazzCash both provide online merchant/payment-gateway routes; PayFast is also a Pakistan-focused gateway option. Final selection must follow the business’s settlement needs, fees, supported cards/wallets, fraud controls and completed merchant onboarding. [Easypaisa merchant onboarding](https://registerbusiness.easypaisa.com.pk/), [JazzCash online merchant onboarding](https://corporateonboarding.jazzcash.com.pk/online-payment), [PayFast’s SBP-approved status in the Pakistan Economic Survey](https://www.finance.gov.pk/survey/chapters_21/08-Trade%20and%20payments.pdf)
- COD risk controls: confirm high-value COD orders via OTP/WhatsApp, limit repeat failed-delivery buyers and require partial/advance payment for selected high-value or pre-order items.
- Use an order-specific bank-transfer reference. Never place raw bank account details inside the frontend source code or a public image.

### Shipping policy defaults to configure—not copy blindly

- Dispatch within 1–2 business days for in-stock products.
- Present a realistic city/zone ETA after entering delivery city.
- State any sale-item delivery charge clearly before checkout.
- State a distinct pre-order dispatch window on the product page, cart and confirmation page.
- International orders: enquiry/checkout path with country, item, shipping quote, duties disclaimer, payment confirmation and no-COD rule.

### Trust and support

- Fixed WhatsApp button on all pages, opening a prefilled non-sensitive support message.
- Show “100% genuine products” only if operations can substantiate it.
- Publish exchange/return eligibility by product type and condition; do not hide the policy in a footer-only page.
- Make policy language easy to read in English; add Urdu summaries for Pakistan-first users.

## 8. Product catalogue model

Every product record needs:

- SKU, collection, category, season, product title, slug, price, compare-at price, tax rule and inventory.
- Photos: cover, front, back, close-up of work, fabric detail and styled shot.
- Video URL(s), alt text and image order.
- Fabric, colour, work type, piece count, stitched state, size chart, model information and care instructions.
- Weight/dimensions for shipping, delivery class, COD eligibility and pre-order flag.
- SEO title, meta description, canonical URL and structured-product data.

Use a controlled vocabulary for fabric and craft: lawn, cambric, organza, chiffon, karandi, silk, cotton, khaddar; embroidery, tilla, sequins, threadwork, adda work, print. This makes filters reliable.

## 9. Build architecture and non-negotiables

### Recommended stack

- **Storefront:** Next.js / React for performance, SEO and a premium custom design.
- **Commerce backend:** Shopify (fastest reliable launch) or WooCommerce (if internal WordPress control is essential). Choose one; do not build a custom order engine in phase one.
- **CMS:** Shopify metaobjects/pages or a headless CMS for campaigns, lookbook and policy content.
- **Payments:** Use hosted/redirected gateway checkout methods approved by the selected merchant provider. The site never stores card data.
- **Shipping:** Courier API/integration after the courier contract, plus manual fallback in the admin.
- **Media:** Optimised WebP/AVIF images; video thumbnails lazy-loaded; embeds consent-aware and performance-tested.

### Quality gates

- Mobile first: tested at 360 px, 390 px, 768 px, 1,024 px and 1,440 px.
- Product/mobile performance: compressed images, lazy loading below fold and no automatic high-bandwidth video playback.
- Accessibility: keyboard navigation, visible focus, image alt text, labels on inputs and sufficient text contrast.
- Security: HTTPS, server-side payment verification, rate limiting on OTP/forms, admin 2FA, backups and least-privilege staff access.
- SEO: crawlable collections, descriptive headings, schema for products/reviews/breadcrumbs, redirects for discontinued products and Search Console/analytics setup.
- Legal: privacy, terms, shipping/returns and cookie/analytics treatment reviewed against the business’s actual practices before launch.

## 10. Delivery roadmap

### Phase 0 — decisions and assets (3–5 working days)

- Confirm product categories, delivery rules, COD rules, payment merchant, return policy and international order approach.
- Supply approved logo source, product data, product images/videos, policy copy, support number/email and social handles.
- Approve one visual moodboard and the palette above.

### Phase 1 — experience and design (1–2 weeks)

- Sitemap, user flows and low-fidelity mobile/desktop wireframes.
- Approved design system, home page, collection page, product page, cart and checkout designs.
- Copy deck for all trust/policy areas.

### Phase 2 — build and integration (2–4 weeks)

- Build storefront, catalogue import, account, cart/checkout, gateway, courier/tracking and CMS.
- Configure transactional messages: order received, payment confirmation, dispatch and delivery.

### Phase 3 — QA and launch (1–2 weeks)

- Test every payment route in sandbox/live approval flow, COD, failed payment, discount, out-of-stock, pre-order, return request and tracking case.
- Test Android Chrome, iPhone Safari and desktop browsers.
- Conduct a soft launch with 10–20 real orders, fix friction, then run the public launch.

## 11. Launch checklist

- [ ] All product prices, stock, sizes and descriptions verified.
- [ ] Every product includes what-is-included, fabric/work and care details.
- [ ] COD eligibility and delivery estimates tested city by city.
- [ ] Payment merchant credentials are live and server-side verification is working.
- [ ] No personal bank details, private customer data or test orders are public.
- [ ] Policies match operational reality.
- [ ] WhatsApp, phone, email, social and tracking links work.
- [ ] Images have alt text; every form and button works by keyboard.
- [ ] Analytics, Search Console, Meta/TikTok pixels (if approved), backup and error monitoring enabled.
- [ ] Team has a simple order-handling and support playbook.

## 12. Features to include later—not in the first launch

- Loyalty points and referrals.
- Multi-currency international checkout once payment settlement is confirmed.
- Personalised recommendations based on behavioural data.
- Live chat beyond WhatsApp.
- Abandoned-cart automation.
- Multi-language full Urdu store.
- Wholesale/B2B portal.

These are valuable only after the catalogue, fulfilment, support and checkout are dependable. The first release should be fast, elegant and easy to operate.
